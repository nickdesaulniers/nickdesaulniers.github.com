Nick Desaulniers is the team lead and engineering manager of the
[Android](https://source.android.com/)
[LLVM](https://llvm.org/) toolchain team at
[Google](https://www.google.com/).  Nick founded the
[ClangBuiltLinux](https://clangbuiltlinux.github.io/) project and served as the
[Linux kernel](https://www.kernel.org/)'s maintainer for
[Clang](https://clang.llvm.org)/[LLVM](https://llvm.org) build support.  He
also worked on the [XLA](https://openxla.org/xla) compiler for
[Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)](https://cloud.google.com/tpu), and
device bringup for
[Nexus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Nexus) and
[Pixel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Pixel) phones.

Nick also worked at [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/) on hardware/software
co-design, compilers, and runtimes for Tesla's two in-house ML architectures for
[Autopilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Autopilot):
[Dojo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Dojo) for training and the
[AI5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Autopilot_hardware#AI5_(Hardware_5))
chip for inference.

At [Mozilla](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/), Nick hacked on open source
projects like
[Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/),
[Firefox OS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firefox_OS),
[Emscripten](https://emscripten.org/),
and
[Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/en-US/).

Nick has twice keynoted the
[US LLVM Developers' Meeting](https://llvm.org/devmtg/), and helps organize
[Linux Plumbers Conference](https://lpc.events/)'s
Toolchain Track,
and the [LLVM Bay Area Monthly Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/llvm-bay-area-social/).

Nick is a proud dad; his hobbies include collecting vinyl records, attending
live music concerts, retro game collecting, watching
[IMSA Sports Car Racing](https://www.imsa.com/), and organizing the
largest [Corvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Corvette)-only
[car show](https://sccorvettes.org/corvette-spectacular/) in Northern
California with his car club, [Santa Clara Corvettes](https://sccorvettes.org/).
